# Performance-based allocation
POST /api/v1/marketing/performance/observations with a confirmed publicationId, cumulative impressions, clicks, conversions and a measurement source (e.g. authorized platform export). Counts are owner-supplied, not fabricated or independently verified. Updates replace the observation, avoiding double-counting. POST /optimize recomputes and GET returns weights. Enable SARAH_OPTIMIZATION_ENABLED for scheduled daily recomputation, default 07:00 Europe/Paris. Minimum 3 posts and 500 impressions; sparse channels remain neutral. Smoothed conversion rates versus a configurable-in-code 1% reference, weights clamped 0.5–2, used by the daily planner. This adjusts channel selection, never ad spend, credentials, approval requirements or post limits. No automatic social engagement ingestion yet; Search Console data is separately available and not attributed to publications automatically.
